Tick a stack to take all its volumes, now and later. Tick volumes one by one to fix the list. Untick a volume under a ticked stack to leave it out of this job. Named volumes only: bind mounts and anonymous volumes are not backed up.

One per line: wharf.backup (any value) or wharf.backup=nightly (this value). A volume that matches any line is taken, and is added to the job when it appears. Labels go in a compose file under the volume (labels:); Docker does not change the labels of a volume that already exists, so a new one has to be created with them.

A 5-field cron expression (0 3 * * * is every night at 3:00, controller time). Empty means manual only.

A database that is writing can give an inconsistent copy when the containers keep running. Stopping them costs a few minutes of downtime and is the safe choice for databases. They are restarted even if the backup fails.

Snapshots per volume. All empty keeps every snapshot, forever. A rule that would remove all of them is refused.
