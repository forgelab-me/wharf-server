Vulnerability scanning

Scans the container images this fleet runs for known vulnerabilities, straight from their registry (by digest, so nothing is pulled onto a host): the digests your image policies applied and could apply next, and every image a container runs, managed by Wharf or not. Results appear as badges next to images; they inform, they do not block anything. Off by default: the scanner and its vulnerability database are downloaded when you turn it on, and the database takes gigabytes of disk.
